ReplayGain

Analyzes audio streams with FFmpeg and applies normalization while Jellyfin transcodes audio. Direct Play remains available until analysis completes.
Measurement method. ebur128 is 5-8 times faster, but unnoticeable less precise.
Uses background FFmpeg analysis for audio streams. Direct Play is disabled only after a valid measurement is available.
Uses a fixed volume adjustment calculated from the measured integrated loudness instead of loudnorm processing. This does not compress the dynamic range. The gain is reduced when necessary to keep the measured true peak within the configured target.
